Privacy

Privacy notice.

A plain-language summary of how the current Mitt's website handles information.

This notice reflects the current static launch website. It will be updated before ecommerce, analytics, advertising or connected email collection is introduced.

Information you provide

Contact and wholesale forms open your email application and send information only when you choose to send the email. The website itself does not store the contents of those forms.

Launch-list forms currently begin an email request. If a connected email platform is introduced, this notice will be updated before subscriptions are collected through the website.

Technical information

The hosting provider may process standard technical information required to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser type, requested pages and security logs. Mitt's Brewing Co. does not currently use its own analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

How information is used

Information sent by email may be used to answer questions, manage wholesale discussions, provide requested launch information and maintain business records.

Sharing

Information is not sold. It may be handled by service providers used to operate email, hosting or business systems, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held by Mitt's Brewing Co., subject to applicable requirements and legitimate business-record obligations.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@mittsbrewing.com.

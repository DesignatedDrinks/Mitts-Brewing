# Mitt's Brewing Co. — Launch Checklist

## Required before the site is live

- [ ] In GitHub, open **Settings → Pages** and choose **GitHub Actions** as the source.
- [ ] In the DNS manager for `mittsbrewing.com`, add the GitHub Pages records shown in GitHub Pages settings.
- [ ] Confirm the custom domain and enable **Enforce HTTPS**.
- [ ] Create `hello@mittsbrewing.com` or replace the email address across the site.
- [ ] Replace mailto-based launch-list forms with the chosen email platform before paid traffic begins.

## Required before product sales

- [ ] Confirm final product ingredients and allergen statement.
- [ ] Confirm final less-than-0.5% alc./vol. wording and bilingual legal copy.
- [ ] Confirm 60 calories, 3 g carbs and 1 g sugar against final lab results and label approval.
- [ ] Confirm where the beer is brewed before retaining “Canadian made” language.
- [ ] Add final case configuration, pricing, shipping and returns information.
- [ ] Connect the Shop page to Shopify or the chosen checkout.
- [ ] Add actual stockists to `find.html`.

## Brand assets still worth commissioning

- [ ] Final custom vector wordmark from a packaging designer.
- [ ] Final one-colour truck icon.
- [ ] Print-ready 355 mL label artwork built from the approved dieline.
- [ ] Professional product photography after the first production run.
- [ ] Wide lifestyle photography for homepage and campaign pages.

## Marketing and measurement

- [ ] Secure matching social handles.
- [ ] Add approved social links to `assets/site.js`.
- [ ] Add Google Search Console and submit `sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Add privacy-safe analytics after updating `privacy.html`.
- [ ] Create a real Open Graph image in PNG or JPG format.

The website intentionally avoids inventing final ingredients, pricing, stockists or production details that have not been confirmed.

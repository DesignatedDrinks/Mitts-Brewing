What carefree
nostalgia means.

A feeling, not a pile of retro props.

Mitt's began with familiar references: an old pickup, a family diner and warm roadside light. But the brand was never meant to become a theme.

The feeling comes first

Carefree nostalgia is nowhere urgent to be, plans made late and enough time for the night to become something else.

We didn't have much planned. We just went.

It is warm and familiar without pretending the past was perfect.

Modern, not costume-retro

Silver, strong typography, sun-faded blue and plenty of space keep Mitt's current. The truck supports the story. The name stays the hero.

The creative test

Does it feel like a modern memory or a themed restaurant? Clean, warm and confident wins.

Where Mitt's belongs

Dinner. Hockey. The dock. A concert. The cooler when the plans are still open.

The beer is non-alcoholic. The feeling is simply a good time.

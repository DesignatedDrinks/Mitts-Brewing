Our story

It started with
an old truck.

The truck was called Mitt. It was imperfect, dependable and always part of the good stories. The name stayed with us because the feeling did too.

The name

A truck called Mitt.

Not polished. Not precious. Just full of character and always ready to go.

Mitt's is not a memorial brand and it is not a truck brand. The truck is simply the door into something larger: the freedom of being young, the people who made ordinary nights matter and the feeling that there was always more road ahead.

The emotional territory

Carefree nostalgia.

Not literal diners, surf shops or gas stations. The warmth behind them.

Those references remind us of open evenings, easy friendships and a time when plans could change without anyone checking the clock. Mitt's translates that feeling into a modern Canadian beer brand.

The feeling

Being in your twenties with nowhere urgent to be and all night to get there.

The visual world

Silver cans, sun-faded blue, cream, warm roadside light and a small truck mark used with restraint.

The beer

Premium non-alcoholic lager that fits naturally into the moment instead of making the moment about what is missing.

The promise

Familiar, well made and easy to bring along.

We didn't have much planned. We just went.
The brand line

Mitt's makes the moment.

The line is not about forcing every occasion to feel important. It is about noticing that the best moments often begin casually.

A cold can. An open seat. A friend who stayed a little longer. That is enough.

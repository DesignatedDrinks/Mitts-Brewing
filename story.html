A truck called Mitt.

Rough around the edges. Reliable enough. Impossible to forget.

Mitt was an old work truck that had seen better days. It rattled, leaked a little oil and never seemed to run perfectly. Somehow, it always found its way into the best stories.

It wasn't special because of what it was. It was special because of what happened around it. Long drives. Wrong turns. Laughter that lasted longer than the inconvenience. The kind of moments that stay with you long after the destination is forgotten.

What Mitt's really means.

Mitt's isn't about looking back. It's about remembering what made those days special in the first place.

The laughter that came out of nowhere. The extra hour you didn't plan to stay. The drive that turned into an adventure. The friends who made ordinary days unforgettable.

We can't bring those moments back. But we can help create new ones.

About Mitt's

Modern beer.
Carefree soul.

Mitt's Brewing Co. is a Canadian non-alcoholic beer brand created by Designated Drinks Inc. We are building something simple, memorable and commercially real.

01

Beer first

We lead with taste, refreshment and recognizable beer styles. The non-alcoholic detail matters, but it does not need to dominate the personality.

02

Restraint

Big typography, exposed silver and one clear colour system. The truck supports the story instead of taking over the package.

03

Good company

Mitt's should always feel welcoming. No lectures, no labels and no pressure to explain what is in your glass.

The company

Built by people who know the category.

Mitt's is a brand of Designated Drinks Inc., a Canadian company focused on making better non-alcoholic choices easier to discover, buy and enjoy.

The company brings real ecommerce, distribution, fulfillment and customer experience knowledge to the brand from day one.

Brand principles

The rules we keep.

Strong brands know what not to do. These principles keep Mitt's from becoming another over-designed craft label.

Never preachy

Talk about the beer and the moment, not what customers should give up.

Never fake-old

Nostalgic warmth without invented dates, fake heritage or excessive distressing.

Never macho

Confident and grounded without becoming aggressive, exclusive or performative.

Never cluttered

The word Mitt's should always be the strongest thing on the shelf.

Made to last.

Classy enough for a restaurant. Easygoing enough for a tailgate. Distinctive enough to become a national brand.
